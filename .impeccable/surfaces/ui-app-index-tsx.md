# Surface brief: the whole web app (front page, story, controversies, outlets, reader)

Scope: every screen of ui/, replaced. Visitor mode: Read (the front page and story pages), Operate (filters, settings).
Audience: Israeli readers across the spectrum, Hebrew, phone first, desktop too.
Owner's brief (pinned, beats any roll): start from zero; drop the illustrations and photos; keep only the idea; a reader should open it and think "wow, this is a news site, and it is comfortable to read"; UX is free; must not look vibe-coded.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Hebrew daily for the screen whose every story is printed with its coverage measured, right against left, and whose two blocs' tellings stand side by side as twin columns. It refuses the app feed of rounded cards, bottom tabs and illustrated guides.

OWN-WORLD: White paper, ink black, rules not boxes. Frank Ruhl Libre for headlines and reading, Assistant for interface and figures. Red and blue are data only: the coverage meter and the side labels. One full-bleed black band per front page, for the blindspots. Sharp corners everywhere, tap highlights as a paper tint.

STORY: The reader sees the day's biggest story and how each side told it before scrolling; learns what one side left out; can open any story to read both tellings and go to every outlet's own article; can explore the controversies, the outlets' positions, and their own reading.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky header (menu, small logotype, search, accessibility) over a section strip. Masthead: logotype, Gregorian and Hebrew date, last update. Then the lead story: serif headline at display size, dek, meta line, the coverage meter at full width, and the right and left summaries as two equal columns.

FORM: Contemporary Hebrew broadsheet, digital (owner-pinned news-site world); signature is the coverage meter (right grows from the right edge, left from the left, unaligned in the middle) on every story at three weights. Seed key: dab46d60 (stage one roll, superseded by the owner's pinned brief).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
